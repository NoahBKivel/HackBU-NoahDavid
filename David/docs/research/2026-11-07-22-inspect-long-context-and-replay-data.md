### Kev's burial procedure (scripts/build_long_states.py, read in full)
- Takes a decision-v7 primary record and inserts random OTHER decision-v7 records of the same partition (never the primary's id) at random positions until the JSON-serialised state reaches the target (1,024/2,048/4,096 tokens by default; up to 1.25x; capped at `MAX_TRAIN_STATE_8K - 64`). New state: `{"records": [...], "primary_record": <1-based slot>, "note": "Answer about the primary record only; the other records are unrelated."}`. Question and label unchanged; src is prefixed `longstate_<length>_`; `_meta` keeps parent_id, primary_slot, state_tokens, length. A matched unburied `train_control.jsonl` is also written.
- Position is uniform random over slots; neighbours are short whole records, so distractors are never evidence for the same question. The model is told which record via the note and `primary_record` index, so it is partly an index lookup, not only reading.
- Defaults: 600 train records per length at 1k/2k/4k. Reaching 16K-32K needs `--lengths 16384,32768` and a raised cap (the script uses the `MAX_TRAIN_STATE_8K` constant from kev.model, about 7.5K).

## 4. What each source would teach, and what must change

- decision-v7 train (replay): preserves general decision skill across typed questions: sentiment/topic/intent classification, NLI, boolq-style yes/no, compositional policy application (conjunction, negation, exception), score scales. Teaches NOTHING about length (max about 460 tokens). Changes: (1) rebalance noul toward 50/50 per source (boolq 64% true, agnews 25% true), or the 41% true prior is replayed; (2) keep minimal-pair groups (`pair_id`, `sibling`, compositional `certificate`) together when subsampling; (3) bury a fraction of replay records at several lengths with the build_long_states mechanism so replayed skills are also exercised at length; (4) stay on the trainable-source list.
- documents-v1 (CFPB): real-document issue/product classification up to about 7K tokens, choice only. Changes: train labels are teacher-consensus (dev/test were judge-verified, train not), so noisier; no noul, so add yes/no variants ("Is the main problem X?"); redaction tokens (XXXX) are a distribution quirk; length skews short (long bucket median 1,326). The complaint is the whole state, so evidence-finding is not exercised: burial or concatenation is needed to make it a long-state task.
- hard-v1 (especially long_policy): multi-step rule application over 1-5K tokens (sections, exclusions, sublimits, arithmetic); the other six families teach short hard reasoning (judge, tradeoff, temporal, probability, ambiguity, multi-hop). Changes: claim sits at the end with a fixed template; for 16-32K, insert unrelated policies or other sections before the claim, put the relevant policy mid-state, and vary the claim position. Generate fresh instances rather than burying the same 857; repeated 5K policies will be memorised. Noul is balanced (47% true).
- QuALITY: long-context reading comprehension, 4-way choice, balanced labels, about half hard, 27% local-lookup. Changes: concatenate other articles for 16-32K with a target pointer and different-story distractors; position robustness means choosing where the target sits (the in-article answer location is unknown); shuffle options; keep the per-article license field and filter to CC BY and Gutenberg if provenance matters; only 150 train and 115 dev articles, so reusing each at several lengths needs different pairings to limit memorisation; test labels are hidden.
- ContractNLI: evidence finding in contracts with absence claims (NotMentioned). Spans enable depth-balanced placement. Changes: NotMentioned -> neutral to reuse the mnli schema; rebalance (12% contradiction); contracts are short, so 16-32K needs 6-13 documents with a party-naming pointer or non-NDA distractors; the 17 repeating hypotheses let a model learn hypothesis priors without reading (balance per hypothesis or paraphrase); keeping it out of training preserves breadth-v1's ContractNLI check.
- CUAD: do not train on it (all 510 contracts sit in longdoc-v1 pools). Use different legal text for training; keep longdoc-v1 CUAD buckets as the untouched read. Kev's published "Validated context" for 0.8B/4B/9B is 8,192 tokens (README line 38 and line 240: "at 16k the measurement on real contracts can no longer rule out a drop of more than 3 points, and at 32k all three are measurably less accurate than at 8k").
- Cross-cutting: Kev's own long records bury short whole records and name the primary one; real long-document tasks do not. For position robustness vary the evidence depth (longdoc-v1 centres the target at 10/50/90% of the state) and balance the label prior per length bucket (longdoc-v1: 120 true / 120 false noul per bucket). README says Kev-27B trained to 32,768-token states and the smaller models mostly to 7,552, so 16K-32K is outside the 4B's trained range; the cap constant `MAX_TRAIN_STATE_8K` in the build script would need to change (how the trainer sets its token cap: UNVERIFIED).
