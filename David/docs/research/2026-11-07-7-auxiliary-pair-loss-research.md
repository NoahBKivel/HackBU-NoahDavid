# Auxiliary pair loss vs CE-on-pairs: research (2026-11-07)

Labels: [M] measured in source as read; [E] my estimate; UNVERIFIED = not checked. NEW = post-cutoff (2026). Budget-limited: ~12 fetches; PDFs/long pages partly truncated.

## VERDICT (top)
NO credible evidence predicts a SUBSTANTIAL (>=+2) gain from an auxiliary pair loss over CE-on-pairs-as-ordinary-examples for Kev. Best direct evidence (PairCFR) = ~+0.3 to +1.0 OOD on 110M-250M encoders; CPA is NOT an auxiliary loss and has an uncontrolled baseline. Nothing found on calibration effects. Optional cheap bet only (see end); expect <=+1 [E], risk to calibration unmeasured.

## 1. CPA (arXiv 2610.41149, https://arxiv.org/html/2610.41149) NEW
- Venue: NOT NeurIPS. Page says workshop "SLMs for Agentic Systems, Paris, 2026"; arXiv v1 30 Oct 2026, cs.AI. NeurIPS 2026 claim UNVERIFIED/contradicted (search found no NeurIPS listing).
- Title: "Counterfactual Pairwise Augmentation Improves Small Pointwise LLM Judges". Qwen3-8B, LoRA r32 a64 dropout .05, all linear, 4 ep, bf16, NEFTune a=5, lr per-criterion {1e-3,5e-4,1e-4}, eff. batch 16 [M].
- Loss form: NO pairwise loss term. Pairwise = a separate SFT dataset D_pair (own prompt "which response satisfies criterion", both orders) concatenated: D_joint = D_point U D_pair; plain causal-LM loss on completion tokens (rationales, 2 per item). "w is not a tuned weight but fixed by composition of the mixture" [M]. Counts ~0.8-1.4k pointwise / ~0.75-1.3k pairwise per criterion.
- Baseline: pointwise-only trained on D_point only. Page does NOT say counterfactual responses were included as pointwise examples; no "pointwise + CF as pointwise" ablation, no matched-data-budget control [M, from read portion; last ~33k chars of page unread]. => KEY CONFOUND UNRESOLVED; the +2.5 conflates extra data volume/task diversity, counterfactual content, and pair format.
- Results macro-F1 (10 inference runs, ONE training seed): pointwise-only 87.9, pairwise-only 75.9, merged 86.6, joint CPA 90.4 (+2.5). Per criterion: C1 71.0->74.2, C2 89.4->91.3, C3 88.6->89.6 (within SD), C4 94.9->99.6, C5 88.8->89.9, C6 94.5->97.7. Gain concentrated in C4/C6 (+4.7/+3.2) [M].
- OOD: none (held-out split from same sources). Calibration: none [M].

## 2. PairCFR (arXiv 2406.06633, ACL 2024; https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06633v1) known-baseline
- Loss: L = lambda*L_CL + (1-lambda)*L_CE; L_CL = InfoNCE on COSINE of HIDDEN REPRESENTATIONS (anchor/positives vs negatives incl. its counterfactual), tau tuned. lambda grid [0,1] step .1; tuned lambda 0.2-0.9 (SA 0.7-0.9; NLI 0.2-0.4); tau 0.07-0.9 [M]. Not a logit/margin loss.
- Models: BERT-base 110M, RoBERTa-base 125M, SBERT 250M, T5-base 223M. 10 seeds SA, 7 NLI [M].
- Baseline HCAD = CE on human-edited CAD (counterfactuals already in as ordinary examples = our exact comparison). Avg OOD acc gain vs HCAD: SA +1.32/+2.22/+0.79/+1.67; NLI +2.90/+2.96/+1.70/+1.24 (BERT/RoBERTa/SBERT/T5) [M]. Includes other tricks (pair batching) — see ablation.
- Ablation (BERT SA OOD avg): ShuffCAD CE 86.34 -> PairCAD CE 86.62 (pair co-batching alone +0.28) -> ShuffCAD CE+CL 87.15 (+0.81) -> PairCAD CE+CL 87.66 (+1.04 over PairCAD CE). So the contrastive term itself ~ +0.8-1.0 on BERT SA [M]. NLI excluding neutral class from CL: 55.59 vs 53.92 including (labels where CF is ambiguous hurt).
- Calibration: not discussed [M]. LLM-scale (4B, LoRA): not tested. In-distribution: not the claim.
- Context: gains are ID-small-to-OOD on weak encoders with tiny CAD sets (~2-3k items); LLM 4B with large mixed data likely less shortcut-prone [E].
- 2025-26 successors found: SALAD 2504.12185 (triplet contrastive w/ margin on LLM-generated counterfactuals; 2025; numbers NOT read, UNVERIFIED), UNMASK 2608.09209 (shortcut diagnosis, not a regularizer), 2608.07838 (counterfactual training for factuality consistency; NOT read). No successor found with LLM-scale head-to-head vs CE-on-augmented.

## 5. Calibration risk
- No source measured calibration effect of a pair/margin term (PairCFR, CPA: none) [M].
- Mechanism [E]: margin/BT/logit-difference terms reward gaps, not absolute probabilities; unbounded margin pushes logits apart => overconfidence, esp. on pairs with authorization-sentence flips where evidence is thin. Docs note BT "says little about absolute score meaning" (https://www.bestaiweb.ai/what-is-reward-model-architecture-and-how-bradley-terry-scoring-shapes-llm-alignment/, secondhand) and a 2026 Reward Lightning (2607.03960) uses tie-aware BT to keep gaps calibrated (not read).
- Mitigations typical [E]: small weight (<=0.1), hinge-capped margin (zero loss once gap >= m, so CE dominates), apply to hidden reps not logits (PairCFR), post-hoc temperature on held-out. Kev already tracks calibration via Brier/label-smoothing options, so ECE regression is detectable.

## 6. Kev trainer (kev/train.py, 680 lines; cloned to scratchpad kev-src; not run)
- question_loss (L41-61): per question, z=logits over options. Soft-target path: -(t*log_softmax(z)).sum(); else F.cross_entropy(z[None],y,label_smoothing); optional brier_w (adds sum-sq prob error), focal_gamma; at most ONE modifier allowed; score q + ord_w adds RPS. Each question's loss summed, / n_questions * v.share, per variant (L352-354).
- batch_loss (L342-365): forward_batch over variants -> logits_b; ce accumulated; anchor KL per variant (L355-358, `--anchor_w`); perm_kl (L359-362). THE hook for a pair term: after L354 loop, where all variants' logits for the micro-batch are in `logits_b`, compute `loss += a.pair_w * pair_term`. Terms logged via `terms` Counter.
- Precedent --perm_kl (L381, L336-338, L359-362): second forward pass of a permuted copy, stored as v.permuted=(enc,perms), encoded in encode_batch, symmetric KL via permutation_kl (L72-76), weight a.perm_kl, fraction a.perm_frac. Incompatible with --row_budget and --pass_tokens_max (L464-468) because the pair shares a loss term across passes.
- Pairing/batching: records are shuffled individually; microbatch_plan (L181+) cuts --batch consecutive records (default --batch 1, --accum 8: optimizer step every 8 micro-batches). Gradients accumulate across micro-batches, so a loss term coupling two records works only if both variants are in the SAME micro-batch (same batch_loss call) — or you use an L2-on-detached-partner trick. none_pairs/record_variants (L280-302) already emit sibling variants (none-present/none-absent minimal pair) in the same record stream => precedent for co-located paired variants. Implementation sketch: give pair records a shared `_meta.pair_id`, have record_variants/microbatch_plan keep partners adjacent in one micro-batch (needs --batch>=2 or sibling mechanism), then in batch_loss match by pair_id (v.request_id/_meta) and add term. Incompatible with --row_budget splitting; --length_sort planning must keep partners together (UNVERIFIED how balanced_runs treats siblings; none-pair siblings are handled via `pairs` arg so reuse that).
- Effort [E]: ~30-50 lines + planner change; moderate risk in microbatch_plan/length_sort.

## Final verdict + if-you-insist recipe
- Plain statement: no, credible evidence does not predict >=+2 over CE-on-pairs for a 4B LoRA model with a decision head. Strongest evidence is +0.3 (pair co-batching) and ~+0.8-1.0 (contrastive term) OOD on 110M-250M encoders (PairCFR, ACL 2024), no calibration data. CPA's +2.5 is a data-format/mixing effect on an 8B judge with an unspecified-confound baseline, workshop paper, 1 training seed; it does not transfer as an "auxiliary loss".
- Best use of the same-context pairs without new loss: (a) co-batch partners (PairCFR: pair batching alone +0.28), (b) exclude ambiguous-label pairs from any pair term (PairCFR neutral finding), (c) evaluate on flip-pair consistency (both-correct rate) — CE-on-pairs likely already lifts it (DISCO).
- If you still add a term (low-risk, expected <=+1 [E], untested for calibration): capped logit-margin on the yes/no (or choice) logit gap for pair partners with opposite labels:
  m_a = z_a[y_a]-logsumexp(z_a[others]) per member; L_pair = mean over pair members of max(0, m0 - m_member)  with m0 ~ 2 (nats), weight pair_w = 0.05-0.1, added on top of unchanged CE (weight 1). Hinge = zero once CE already separates, so it only acts on confused pairs and cannot inflate confidence beyond m0. Alternative consistent with PairCFR: InfoNCE on pooled pointer-head hidden states, lambda ~0.1-0.3 in the (1-lambda)CE form (PairCFR tuned lambda 0.2-0.9 for encoders; transfer to LoRA 4B UNVERIFIED). Gate: run A/B at 2 seeds, require >= +1 on flip-pair consistency AND ECE/Brier not worse than +0.005 before keeping; fit temperature on held-out afterward either way.
